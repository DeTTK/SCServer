SC Vehicles adaptive sound bank 0.9.0

This bank is an acoustic approximation by engine/drivetrain family. The donor
recordings are not claimed to be recordings of the exact Soviet vehicle models.
Processed assets, hashes, source URLs and modifications are recorded in
results/audio-assets-0.9.0.json and tools/prepare-audio.py.

INSTALLED SC FOLEY:
diesel-clatter, start-diesel, stop, failed-start, enter, exit, gravel, soil, sand, snow, track
are selected processed excerpts from this user's installed SC assets at
the installed game modassets/assets directory. Original game asset rights remain with their
respective owners. They are not relicensed as CC0 or CC BY by this mod.
Exact paths and source hashes are in the asset manifest. Local conversion:
mono PCM, filtered, short excerpts, normalized, loops crossfaded where needed.
